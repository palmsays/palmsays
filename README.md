# PalmSays

Palmistry is a cultural practice in which the lines, shapes, and features of the hand are studied as symbols within different communities and historical traditions. Across regions, people have used palm reading to share stories, compare inherited interpretations, and reflect on how meaning is made. Its approaches vary, and it is often treated as folklore and cultural expression rather than as a scientific method. This guide introduces major hand areas, common terminology, and ways palmistry has been discussed in everyday life.
